Hypertension is a chronic medical condition in which the blood pressure is persistently elevated. It is a major risk factor for stroke, myocardial infarction and chronic kidney disease. Most people with hypertension have no symptoms. Hypertension is classified as primary or secondary.

Primary hypertension is caused by a combination of genetic and lifestyle factors. Lifestyle factors include high salt intake, obesity and physical inactivity. Antihypertensive drugs are used to treat hypertension. Thiazide diuretics are often the first-line treatment. Reducing salt intake and increasing exercise can prevent hypertension.
